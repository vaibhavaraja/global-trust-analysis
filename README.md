 Global Trust Analysis

A data analysis project for COMM 187 at UCSB exploring how structural factors and individual traits relate to trust across countries, using the Global Preferences Survey (briq Institute).

## Research Question
How do structural factors, like linguistic diversity, and individual traits, like risk-taking and altruism, relate to differences in trust across countries?

## Hypotheses
1. Countries where more languages are spoken have lower trust scores (Amelia Fischer)
2. Higher trust is correlated with higher risk-taking (Vaibhava Rajesh)
3. People with higher trust also have higher altruism (Bonnie Yuan)

## Findings
- **H1: Supported.** Multilingual countries had significantly lower average trust (t = 2.16, p = 0.034).
- **H2: Not supported.** Trust and risk-taking had almost no relationship (r = 0.036).
- **H3: Supported.** The high-trust group showed higher average altruism than the low-trust group.
